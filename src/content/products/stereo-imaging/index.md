Our compact underwater stereo imaging system captures precise, three-dimensional images of underwater environments. It consists of a pair of synchronised cameras behind glass domes that collect detailed 3D scene imagery.

## Key features

- **Stereo camera pair:** two high-resolution, low-light cameras capture synchronised images for 3D reconstruction of seafloor structures, marine habitats or underwater assets.
- **Waterproof housing:** pressure-resistant, corrosion-proof housing rated for 300 m, with deeper ratings available in customised designs.
- **Integrated lighting:** LED or laser-based illumination enhances image quality in low light, reducing shadows and backscatter.
- **Compact, low-power design:** small, lightweight construction for easy integration with AUVs, ROVs or diver-operated systems.
- **Onboard processing and storage:** real-time image processing and efficient data storage for quick analysis and post-mission review.

## Applications

- **Seafloor mapping and habitat monitoring:** 3D reconstruction of coral reefs, benthic ecosystems and underwater structures.
- **Asset inspection:** detailed imaging of pipelines, cables and submerged infrastructure.
- **Archaeology and search:** precise documentation of underwater wrecks and artefacts.
