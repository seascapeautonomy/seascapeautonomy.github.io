In October 2025, Gideon, Jay, Nadav and Stefan spent time at Lizard Island in the far north of the Great Barrier Reef deploying the Seeker AUVs and a small imaging ROV over reefs. This is part of a long-term monitoring program that the group has been running at sites around the island.
